# Driver Allowance and Overnight Halt Policy

Document code: DAP
Issued by: Human Resources and Operations, Sahyadri Freight Carriers Private Limited,
Gut No. 217/3, Village Kalher, Bhiwandi–Kalyan Road, Bhiwandi, Dist. Thane 421302.
Applicability: All drivers on the rolls of the Company and all depot managers at the
Bhiwandi Central Hub (BWD), Panvel Cross-dock (PNU), Nashik Satellite Depot (NSK) and
Vapi Border Depot (VPI).

This Policy governs the payment of road allowance, night-out allowance and trunk
incentive to drivers, and the conditions under which an overnight halt away from base is
permitted. It is to be read with the Fuel Card and Reimbursement Standard Operating
Procedure (FCR), the Accident and Breakdown Reporting Procedure (ABR) and the Preventive
Maintenance Schedule (PMS).

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this Policy, unless the context otherwise requires —

(a) "Company" means Sahyadri Freight Carriers Private Limited, including every depot
listed above.

(b) "Depot Manager" means the officer in administrative control of a depot, or a person
duly authorised by him in writing, who sanctions duty rosters and passes allowance
claims for that depot.

(c) "Duty Period" means the period from the moment a driver is required to report for a
trip until he signs off at the depot on completion of that trip, inclusive of loading,
unloading and statutory attendance but exclusive of any period during which he is at
liberty to dispose of his time.

(d) "Inter-duty Rest" means the continuous period between the termination of one Duty
Period and the commencement of the next, during which the driver is not at the disposal
of the Company.

(e) "Statutory Rest" means the minimum daily rest interval prescribed for an adult motor
transport worker under the Motor Transport Workers Act, 1961.

(f) "Overnight Halt" means an authorised stoppage of a driver away from his base depot
that requires him to rest overnight before resuming duty.

(g) "Road Allowance" means the flat daily sum payable to a driver for each day of a
Duty Period, on account of subsistence away from base, and is distinct from any night-out
allowance.

(h) "Night-out Allowance" means the additional sum payable for each night for which an
Overnight Halt is authorised and actually taken.

(i) "Driver Grade" means the grade DG-1, DG-2 or DG-3 to which a driver is assigned, and
"Route Class" means the classes R1, R2 and R3 by which trips are categorised for the
purposes of this Policy.

## 2. Scope and grades

2.1 Every driver is assigned to exactly one Driver Grade, and the grade determines both
the classes of vehicle he may drive and the rates payable to him under Section 4.

2.2 The grades, their eligibility and their route eligibility are as follows.

| Grade | Minimum eligibility | Vehicle classes | Route classes |
|-------|---------------------|-----------------|---------------|
| DG-1 (Feeder Driver) | Valid LMV-Transport licence; not less than 2 years' commercial experience | A | R1 only |
| DG-2 (Staff Driver) | Valid HMV licence; not less than 4 years' commercial experience | A, B, C | R1, R2, R3 |
| DG-3 (Senior Trunk Driver) | Valid HMV licence with multi-axle endorsement; not less than 7 years' commercial experience | A, B, C, D | R1, R2, R3 |

2.3 A DG-1 driver is rostered on R1 (local and feeder) duties only and shall not be
assigned to a Route Class R2 or R3. Since an R1 trip carries no Overnight Halt, no DG-1
driver becomes eligible for Night-out Allowance in the ordinary course.

2.3(a) Notwithstanding clause 2.3, a Depot Manager may, by written waiver recording the
operational reason, assign a DG-1 driver to a single R2 trip; in every such case the
driver's Inter-duty Rest shall remain governed by DAP-3.2 and no relaxation of that
clause is permitted by reason of the waiver.

## 3. Hours of duty and rest

3.1 The spread-over of a driver's Duty Period on any day shall not be arranged so as to
exceed the limit fixed for an adult motor transport worker under Section 16 of the Motor
Transport Workers Act, 1961, and the aggregate hours of work shall be kept within the
limits fixed under Section 13 of that Act.

3.2 The Company fixes its own minimum Inter-duty Rest at **11 hours** of continuous rest
between the termination of duty on any one day and the commencement of duty on the next.
This internal minimum is deliberately set above the statutory floor of nine consecutive
hours prescribed under Section 15(2) of the Motor Transport Workers Act, 1961; where the
two differ, the higher figure of 11 hours governs the roster and no driver shall be
called for duty until it has elapsed.

3.3 A Depot Manager shall so construct the roster that the Inter-duty Rest under DAP-3.2
is capable of being taken at the base depot or at an authorised halting station, and not
merely on paper.

3.4 A driver shall not be required to drive for more than **4 hours 20 minutes** of
continuous driving without a break of not less than **40 minutes**, during which he is
relieved of all driving and attendance duty. A break taken to comply with this clause
does not by itself create an entitlement to Night-out Allowance.

## 4. Road allowance, night-out allowance and incentive

4.1 Road Allowance is payable at the following flat daily rates for each day of a Duty
Period, according to the driver's grade.

| Grade | Road Allowance per day |
|-------|------------------------|
| DG-1 | Rs. 342 |
| DG-2 | Rs. 476 |
| DG-3 | Rs. 615 |

4.2 Road Allowance is payable on account of subsistence only and is separate from, and
in addition to, any fuel drawn on the Company fuel card, which is governed exclusively
by FCR-3 of the Fuel Card and Reimbursement Standard Operating Procedure. A driver shall
not set off Road Allowance against a fuel-card drawing, nor a fuel-card drawing against
Road Allowance.

4.3 Night-out Allowance is payable, for each authorised night of Overnight Halt actually
taken, at **Rs. 638** for a DG-2 driver and **Rs. 840** for a DG-3 driver. A DG-1 driver
has no Night-out Allowance rate, consistent with DAP-2.3.

4.4 A DG-3 driver who completes a Route Class R3 (trunk / inter-state) trip is entitled
to a trunk-trip completion incentive of **Rs. 1,120** for each such completed trip, in
addition to Road Allowance and any Night-out Allowance. The incentive is not payable on
an R1 or R2 trip and is not payable to any other grade.

4.5 Allowance and incentive claims are passed only against a Duty Period that has been
closed on the system; the timing and evidentiary requirements for a related fuel claim
are those set out in FCR-6.1 of the Fuel Card and Reimbursement Standard Operating
Procedure, and a driver should not assume that closure of one claim closes the other.

## 5. Overnight halt eligibility

5.1 An Overnight Halt is not permissible on a trip whose one-way distance is less than
**186 km**. That distance is the floor of Route Class R2; a trip below it is a Route
Class R1 trip, for which a same-day return to base is expected and no halt is authorised.

5.2 On a Route Class R2 trip (one-way distance of 186 km up to and including 640 km), not
more than one Overnight Halt may be authorised, and only where the Inter-duty Rest under
DAP-3.2 cannot be completed at the base depot within the running of the trip.

5.3 On a Route Class R3 trip (one-way distance above 640 km), such number of Overnight
Halts may be authorised as the running of the trip genuinely requires, and Night-out
Allowance is payable for each night under DAP-4.3.

5.4 Night-out Allowance is payable for an authorised Overnight Halt on a Route Class R2
trip: Provided that where the Inter-duty Rest is in fact completed at a Company depot
during the running of that trip, the halt is treated as not having been necessary and no
Night-out Allowance accrues, notwithstanding that the driver remained away from base
overnight.

5.5 Where a halt becomes necessary by reason of a breakdown rather than the roster, the
driver's first obligation is to report and secure the vehicle in accordance with ABR-3
and ABR-5 of the Accident and Breakdown Reporting Procedure, and the question of
Night-out Allowance is decided only after that report is on record.

## 6. Interaction with maintenance and dispatch

6.1 A driver shall not be rostered onto a vehicle that stands withdrawn from service for
maintenance under PMS-7 of the Preventive Maintenance Schedule; a Duty Period commenced
on such a vehicle does not attract allowance under this Policy.

6.2 Where a trip is curtailed because a vehicle is taken into the shop mid-route, Road
Allowance is payable for each day actually spent on duty up to the curtailment, and the
trunk incentive under DAP-4.4 is not payable unless the R3 trip is in fact completed.

## 7. Records and audit

7.1 Every allowance and incentive claim shall be supported by the closed Duty Period
record, the authorised roster and, where a halt is claimed, the Depot Manager's
authorisation. Records under this Policy shall be retained for not less than 5 years and
produced on demand to an inspector appointed under the Motor Transport Workers Act, 1961.

7.2 A claim found to rest on a roster that breaches DAP-3.2 shall be held and referred to
Human Resources before payment, and the breach shall be recorded against the depot.
