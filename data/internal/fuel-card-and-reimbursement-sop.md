# Fuel Card and Reimbursement Standard Operating Procedure

Document code: FCR
Issued by: Finance and Operations, Sahyadri Freight Carriers Private Limited, Gut No.
217/3, Village Kalher, Bhiwandi–Kalyan Road, Bhiwandi, Dist. Thane 421302.
Applicability: All drivers holding a Company fuel card, all depot managers and the fuel
desk at each depot (BWD, PNU, NSK, VPI).

This Procedure governs the issue and use of the Company fuel card, the fuel norms against
which consumption is measured, and the reimbursement of fuel and incidental expenditure.
It is to be read with the Driver Allowance and Overnight Halt Policy (DAP), the Accident
and Breakdown Reporting Procedure (ABR) and the Preventive Maintenance Schedule (PMS).

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this Procedure, unless the context otherwise requires —

(a) "Fuel Card" means the fleet fuel card issued in the name of the Company and mapped to
a single vehicle, by which diesel is drawn at authorised outlets.

(b) "Fuel Norm" means the reference fuel efficiency, expressed in kilometres per litre,
fixed for a vehicle class under FCR-4.2, against which actual consumption on a trip is
measured.

(c) "Actual Consumption" means the fuel drawn on the Fuel Card for a trip divided into
the distance run on that trip, as recorded from the opening and closing odometer.

(d) "Variance" means the proportionate shortfall of Actual Consumption below the Fuel
Norm for the class, expressed as a percentage of the Norm.

(e) "Pre-authorisation" means the prior written sanction of the Depot Manager for a
single fuel-card transaction that would otherwise exceed the ceiling in FCR-3.3.

(f) "Trip Close" means the point at which a trip is marked completed on the system with
its closing odometer, closing fuel and route recorded.

(g) "Reimbursement Claim" means a claim by a driver or depot for fuel or incidental
expenditure met out of pocket rather than on the Fuel Card.

(h) "Overloading" means the carriage of goods such that the laden weight of the vehicle
exceeds the permissible weight for that vehicle, within the meaning of the Motor Vehicles
Act, 1988.

## 2. Scope

2.1 A Fuel Card is mapped to one vehicle and may be operated only by the driver to whom
that vehicle stands allotted for the trip. A drawing on a Fuel Card mapped to another
vehicle is irregular and shall be recovered.

2.2 Fuel drawn on the Fuel Card is distinct from Road Allowance, which is a subsistence
payment governed by DAP-4.1 of the Driver Allowance and Overnight Halt Policy; neither is
to be set off against the other.

## 3. Issue and limits of the fuel card

3.1 A Fuel Card is issued by the fuel desk against the vehicle record and is de-activated
automatically when the vehicle is withdrawn from service under PMS-7 of the Preventive
Maintenance Schedule.

3.2 The monthly drawing limit on a Fuel Card is fixed by vehicle class as follows.

| Class | Monthly fuel-card limit |
|-------|-------------------------|
| A | Rs. 41,900 |
| B | Rs. 88,400 |
| C | Rs. 1,63,200 |
| D | Rs. 2,14,600 |

3.3 A single fuel-card transaction shall not exceed **Rs. 14,750** without Pre-authorisation.
A drawing above that figure without prior written sanction of the Depot Manager is
irregular, is recovered from the person who authorised it, and is reported to Finance.

3.4 The monthly limit in FCR-3.2 and the per-transaction ceiling in FCR-3.3 operate
together; sanction of a single transaction under FCR-3.3 does not enlarge the monthly
limit for the class.

## 4. Fuel norms and variance

4.1 Actual Consumption on every trip is computed at Trip Close from the recorded distance
and the fuel drawn, and is compared with the Fuel Norm for the vehicle's class.

4.2 The Fuel Norm by vehicle class is as follows.

| Class | Fuel Norm |
|-------|-----------|
| A | 8.60 km per litre |
| B | 5.40 km per litre |
| C | 3.85 km per litre |
| D | 3.05 km per litre |

4.3 The Fuel Norm is a reference figure for monitoring only and does not cap the fuel a
driver may draw for a genuine trip; it is the basis on which Variance is calculated under
FCR-4.4.

4.4 A Variance of the Actual Consumption below the Fuel Norm of more than **7.5%** shall
be placed before the Depot Manager for review, and the driver shall account for it.
Fuel drawn on the Fuel Card for a trip is reimbursable in the ordinary course:
Notwithstanding the foregoing, where the trip is subsequently found to have been run in
Overloading such that an offence under Section 194(1) of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 is
made out, the fuel drawn on that trip ceases to be a Company expense and the whole of it
is recovered from the driver, however small the Variance.

## 5. Overloading and permissible weight

5.1 A driver shall not accept or carry a load that causes the vehicle to exceed the
permissible weight fixed for it, the limits of weight being those laid down under Section
113 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.

5.2 A finding of Overloading under FCR-4.4 shall be entered against the trip and copied to
the Accident and Breakdown Reporting record where any accident on that trip is involved,
so that ABR-2 of the Accident and Breakdown Reporting Procedure reflects the load
condition of the vehicle at the material time.

## 6. Reimbursement claims and deadline

6.1 A Reimbursement Claim shall be submitted, with the fuel receipt and the closed trip
record, within **9 calendar days** of Trip Close. A claim submitted after that period is
time-barred and shall not be passed except on the written condonation of Finance recording
the reason for delay.

6.2 A Reimbursement Claim for expenditure of a type recorded as a trip expense (toll,
parking, fine or maintenance) shall be classified accordingly; a fine met by a driver is
reimbursable only where the Company accepts liability for it, and a fine arising from
Overloading under FCR-4.4 is not reimbursable.

6.3 A claim relating to a breakdown, including any on-spot recovery met by the driver, is
governed by ABR-5 of the Accident and Breakdown Reporting Procedure and shall be routed
through that Procedure and not as an ordinary fuel claim under this Section.

## 7. Records and audit

7.1 Fuel-card statements, Trip Close records and Reimbursement Claims shall be retained
for not less than 7 years and reconciled monthly by the fuel desk against the vehicle
records.

7.2 A driver whose trips show repeated Variance beyond FCR-4.4 without satisfactory
account, or repeated irregular drawings under FCR-3.3, shall be referred to Operations and
his fitness to hold a Fuel Card reviewed.
